# Staged credit-history and model comparison

246,008 development applications; 19,860 positives (8.07%); 5 matched folds, seed 42, 2 native threads. Holdout excluded.

HGB max_iter 1000; LightGBM trees 1000; learning rate 0.05; inner-split patience 30.

## What changed

- Previous-application history improved mean fold AUC by **+0.003811 for HGB** and **+0.003869 for LightGBM**, positive in all five folds for each algorithm. Retain it as a research candidate.
- The application-feature bundle showed no clear gain: mean fold AUC changes were -0.000229 and -0.000523; all its conditional pooled AUC/AP intervals include zero. A direct previous-history-without-this-bundle ablation remains untested.
- The full-feature algorithms are effectively tied on ranking: HGB 0.780062 versus LightGBM 0.780078 mean fold AUC. HGB has higher pooled AP (0.270274 versus 0.269765), while LightGBM uses about 27% of HGB's fit time (288.5 versus 1,082.3 seconds, including selection and refitting). The algorithm-difference conditional intervals include zero.
- At top-5% capacity, LightGBM flags 12,300 applications, catches 4,430 positives and flags 7,870 negatives: 36.02% precision and 22.31% recall. HGB catches 4,420 at that capacity but has better top-10% precision. These are descriptive tradeoffs, not lending-policy or profit estimates.
- All 50 searches stopped below the 1,000-round ceiling. Total run time was 6,018.7 seconds.

- All estimates use previously inspected development data; holdout is not scored.
- Early stopping and its preprocessing use only a stratified inner-training split; the chosen length is refitted on the full outer training fold. Outer validation is excluded.
- Bootstrap intervals condition on fixed OOF scores and label prevalence; they omit refitting, overlapping-fold dependence, selection and population shift. They are not generalization confidence intervals or significance tests.
- Installment features describe observed past-payment tranches, not every unpaid obligation.
- Ambiguous schedules/duplicate events are conservatively excluded; snapshots need provenance review.
- Top-k policies are descriptive capacity comparisons, not tuned lending decisions.

Aggregate per-fold metrics, paired feature/algorithm deltas, operating-point counts, audits and feature schema are saved in data/, with code/input hashes and versions in the report manifest. Applicant-level assignments and predictions remain in the ignored local output directory.


## Interpretation and reproducibility

This is one fixed, prespecified 1,000-round / learning-rate 0.05 / patience-30 protocol on the original five development folds. Both algorithms select length on the same seeded inner split with isolated preprocessing, then refit on the full outer training fold. The October configuration also differed in stopping, refitting and HGB learning rate: any cross-run difference cannot be attributed solely to larger budgets. Within this run, adjacent stages hold the protocol fixed.

The external-score stage also includes three unrelated application ratios; its change belongs to the whole bundle. Previous approved amounts are nominal historical approvals, not current debt. There are no day-zero decisions in the source previous-application table; <0 and <=0 give identical features here. Snapshot availability is still not independently established.

Mean fold AUC is the selection metric. The linked bootstrap audits estimate pooled OOF AUC/AP changes, condition on fixed scores and fold/class counts, and omit refitting, overlapping-fold dependence, selection and population shift. They are not generalization confidence intervals or significance tests. The original inspected holdout is not scored. No independent final data, calibrator, tuned policy, profit estimate, final model or submission is produced.

Aggregate tables are in data/. Row-level assignments, predictions, checkpoints and raw data remain in the ignored local output directory. Exact package pins are in requirements-experiment.txt; code/input/version manifests identify this run.
