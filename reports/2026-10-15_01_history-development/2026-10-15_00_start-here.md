# History features and fixed-model comparison

[Report](2026-10-15_02_report.md) · [Compact tables](data/) · [Manifest](2026-10-15_04_manifest.json)

Six configured pipelines use the same 246,008 original development applications and five folds. The holdout is excluded. HGB plus bureau and installment features reached mean fold AUC 0.773351 and OOF AP 0.260517. Combined LightGBM was faster, with slightly lower AUC/AP and slightly higher precision at top 5% and 10%. Its top-5% precision was 34.98%, with 21.67% recall. These are development estimates, not fresh validation or an operational lending policy.
